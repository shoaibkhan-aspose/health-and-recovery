      Crawl audit per host
      Speed per subdomain

      Whole host: real Chrome users over 28 days, 75th percentile, mobile (good: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1). Pages: the most-visited pages per template; "real-user data" counts pages with enough visits for their own numbers. The lab score (Lighthouse, 0-100) varies by up to ~20 points between runs, so it is shown here only, never as a finding.

      AI access per subdomain

      Can AI search and answer engines (ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Copilot) reach each host? "robots.txt" and "Firewall" test their crawlers; a block there is a finding. Blocks on AI training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended) are a business choice, shown here only. llms.txt is a proposed convention (llmstxt.org) that some AI tools read: on products, a family counts as covered by its own file or a link from the host's file.
