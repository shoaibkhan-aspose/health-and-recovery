Health score (0–100)

health = 100 − Σ weight × share
SeverityWeight
Broken40
Incorrect20
Not optimized6
Improvable2
  • share = part of the family or subdomain affected, at least 25%
  • one root cause counts once; not optimized + improvable cost at most 20 together
  • a subdomain with product families: their scores weighted by clicks, minus subdomain-wide problems
  • traffic is not part of health: a small family's broken pages count as much as a big one's
  • health by area: the same formula on one area's findings, so the overall is never higher than an area; speed without real-user data shows no score
below 4040–5960–7475–8990+

Example: a broken finding on 25% of a subdomain costs 40 × 0.25 = 10 points: score 90.

Reports not yet on the new layout also show a letter: A 90+, B 75–89, C 60–74, D 40–59, F below 40.

Priority score (0–100)

score = traffic × severity ÷ effort
  • traffic: Search Console clicks on the affected pages over 28 days, log scale (impressions count 1% of a click)
  • if the same pages lost traffic since six months ago and the loss is larger, half the loss counts
Severity×Effort÷
Broken1S: one template or setting1
Incorrect0.7M: several templates1.5
Not optimized0.4L: rebuild or migration2.5
Improvable0.2

Health says how broken; the score says what to fix first.

Limits

  • Samples: page checks run on 4 pages per template; "~N pages affected" scales the failing share up to the template's URLs
  • Confidence on each finding: complete, strong, partial or to confirm (one page)
  • Weights (40 / 20 / 6 / 2) are set by judgement, not yet checked against traffic changes
  • Lab speed scores vary by up to 20 points between runs, so they are never a finding
  • LLM findings are reviewed by hand; false positives are removed
  • All audits only read; nothing on any site is changed

Glossary

Sitemap / indexXML list of a site's pages for search engines; an index lists other sitemaps
Canonicalthe address a page names as its preferred one; Google indexes that address
hreflangtags linking the language versions of a page; x-default is the fallback
noindexasks search engines to keep a page out of results
llms.txta short file at /llms.txt telling AI assistants what a site is; a proposal (llmstxt.org), not a standard
Structured datamachine-readable facts in a page (JSON-LD) for search and answer engines
Redirect 301 / 302a URL forwarding to another; 301 permanent, 302 temporary
Soft 404an error page that answers "OK" (HTTP 200) instead of "not found"
Templatea URL pattern shared by many pages, e.g. /cells/conversion/<a>-to-<b>
Familya product section of products.aspose.app, e.g. /words/
Clicks / impressionsSearch Console: how often a page was clicked / shown in Google results
LCP, INP, CLSCore Web Vitals: load speed (good ≤ 2.5 s), response to input (≤ 200 ms), layout shift (≤ 0.1)
